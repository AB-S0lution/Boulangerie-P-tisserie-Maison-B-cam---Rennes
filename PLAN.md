# Maison Bécam Rennes (Boulangerie Pâtisserie Maison Bécam - Rennes): Research & Plan

> **Maison Bécam Rennes** is the first Brittany shop of Maison Bécam, a boulangerie-pâtisserie franchise network founded in Angers in 2005 by Cécile and Nicolas Bécam. It opened on **22 March 2025** at **69 rue Jean Guéhenno, 35700 Rennes**, at the foot of the **Jules Ferry metro station (line B)** and across from the Université de Rennes Campus Centre (law faculty, Sciences Po, IGR, about 16,000 students nearby). It is a big **300 m² "lieu de vie"**: bread, viennoiserie, pâtisserie, snacking, an aperitif range and a salon de thé with about 50 seats. Bakers work **behind a large glass wall** in full view of customers, and the shop is **open every day from 7:00 to 20:00**. The operating company is **BB FERRY SAS**, presided over by Marina Prime. The press presented Marina Prime and Jean-Manuel Xambo as the franchisee couple, both career-changers. Customers give it **4.6/5 on Google from 1,051 reviews**. They praise the warm welcome, the baguette and flûte, the kouign-amann, the 7/7 hours ("la seule boulangerie ouverte un jour férié") and how affordable it is for students. Its signature is the network's **Choc'O Roi**: chocolate Breton-style sablés, 65% cocoa pastry cream, Guérande salt, topped with a **fleur de lys**. In short, the network brings royal Angevin navy-and-gold to a Rennes campus metro exit. The site has to show it as both a trusted national name and a real neighbourhood bakery you can watch through the glass.

## 2. Story & history

### The network (credit everything here to "le réseau Maison Bécam", never to the Rennes shop)
Source: https://maisonbecam.com/pages/notre-histoire unless stated.
- **2005**: Cécile Bécam (a background in gastronomy and wine) and Nicolas Bécam (from a family of bakers; he "shaped his first croissant at age 3") found the business at the **Boulangerie St Julien in Angers**.
- **1998** (before): Nicolas Bécam was **Meilleur Apprenti Boulanger du Maine-et-Loire**.
- **2012**: pastry workshop created. **Farine Maison Bécam** created with **Minoterie Girardeau**, the flour behind the "Flûte Bécam".
- **3 Feb 2013**: **Choc'O Roi** launched at the Château du Roi René in Angers. That year the headquarters also moved to Saint-Barthélemy-d'Anjou. The Choc'O Roi later carried the "**Produit en Anjou**" label (https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/maison-becam-le-choco-roi-labellise-produit-en-anjou-104313).
- **2014**: e-shop.
- **2015**: **1st**, best croissant of Maine-et-Loire. That year also brought a national 2nd place for an apprentice.
- **2017**: **2nd and 3rd**, best croissant of Maine-et-Loire.
- **2020**: first expansion outside Anjou, to Nantes.
- **2021**: recognition for apprenticeship and diversity.
- **March 2022**: new central pastry workshop of **1,200 m²**. Pastries are "fabriquées par nos pâtissiers dans notre nouvel atelier de pâtisserie" and delivered to the shops (https://maisonbecam.com/pages/mieux-decouvrir-notre-concept-maison-becam).
- **30 Nov 2024**: **Capital × Statista "Meilleure Boulangerie de France 2025"**, ranked 1st among bakery chains, **8.35/10**, from a survey of 20,000 consumers over 36 days in 2024 (https://www.toute-la-franchise.com/news-maison-becam-elue-meilleure-boulangerie-de-france-2025-par-capital-magazine). This is a **chain ranking based on customer experience**, not a baking contest, and it does not apply specifically to Rennes.
- **10 Apr 2025**: the network celebrates its **20th anniversary** (snacking.fr; L'Express Franchise "20 ans d'histoire et d'artisanat"). Same article: standard 300 m² format targets **€1M turnover within 18 months**; central workshop **1,300 m², €3.5M**; 2025 forecast revenue €36M (https://www.snacking.fr/actualites/7474-boulangeries-maison-becam-lancent-deux-nouveaux-formats-boutiques-pour-soutenir-croissance/). It sits « au pied du métro Jules Ferry » and « face à une bouche de métro », on the **ground floor of a new building** (rennes-scope), « face à la faculté de droit » (actu.fr). The owners' own framing (Ouest-France): a **commerce de proximité** answering « une demande forte des habitants » and the City's push to « freiner les déplacements interquartiers ». That is a strong, real "neighbourhood" line for the site's quartier page.
- **Bus at the door (OSM, Jul 2026):** stop « Jules Ferry » = C2, 10, N5, BreizhGo 504; « Sévigné » / « Vincennes » (~250-300 m) add C3 and 12. See §1.

- **Campus neighbours:** the **Faculté de droit et de science politique** (9 rue Jean Macé, ~150 m, ~4,800 students: https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/structures-enseignement/bretagne/ille-et-vilaine/faculte-de-droit-et-science-politique-universite-de-rennes), Sciences Po Rennes, IGR, the **Archives municipales**, **Lycée Jean-Macé**.   - **Rennes coat of arms:** *palé d'argent et de sable, au chef d'hermine* (vertical silver/black stripes, ermine chief: https://heraldry-wiki.com/wiki/Rennes). A single **ermine moucheture** works as a bullet or separator.
  - **Purple schist of Pont-Réan + brick:** the burnt tones of old Rennes walls (https://www.pariszigzag.fr/rennes-actu/rennes-medieval/, https://m.shabretagne.com/scripts/files/63cfa6e5912845.68988270/2016_01.pdf). An aubergine-earth tone that also reads as a crust colour.
  - **Pans de bois:** ~270 half-timbered houses survived the 1720 fire, which suggests a timber-frame grid.
  - **Coucou de Rennes:** the local heritage hen with blue-grey plumage and dark "cuckoo" barring, saved by the Écomusée (https://www.reussir.fr/volailles/la-coucou-de-rennes-se-raconte, https://www.poules-racesdefrance.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/17_COUCOU-DE-RENNES.pdf). A barred blue-grey pattern that sits naturally with Bécam blue.

  - **Anjou meets Brittany:** the shop is the network's *first in Brittany*. **Fleur de lys (Anjou) + ermine (Rennes/Bretagne)** is the honest local story.
  - **Avoid:** triskells, bigoudène coiffes, lighthouses, marinière stripes, "Breizh" puns, Gwenn-ha-Du flags, crêpes.

Within 1 km it is the **only 300 m² bakery-café with ~50 seats and continuous 7/7, 7:00-20:00 hours, at a metro exit on a university campus**. Most neighbours are small artisans with split hours or chef-led boutiques. The site must:
1. **Show the craft through the glass.** Bakers visible, bread made on site. This answers the "chain = industrial" objection.
2. **Sell the whole day.** Breakfast, lunch (formules dès 7,90 €), goûter, evening bread, on Sundays and public holidays too.
3. **Carry national trust lightly.** Capital × Statista 2025 (credited to the network) and 1,000+ Google reviews.
4. **Feel local.** First Bécam in Brittany, Breton specialities, a neighbourhood team, campus and Thabor.
5. **Remove friction.** Click & Collect up front to answer the queue complaint, live open status, metro directions.

## 9. Design direction

Design skills consulted: **ui-ux-pro-max** (design system query "bakery patisserie tea room heritage premium", variance 6 / motion 8 / density 4) and **design-taste-frontend**. From ui-ux-pro-max I kept: the **Scroll-Triggered Storytelling** pattern (chapters, end-of-chapter mini CTA, a reduced-motion state that renders each chapter final and readable), the **Cormorant + Montserrat** "Luxury Serif" pairing (it keeps the brand's real Montserrat), the "avoid cheap visuals / fast animations" rule and Flip-based shared-element transitions. I **rejected** its "Liquid Glass" style suggestion (an Apple-platform material; it would read as generic glassmorphism) and its black + brass palette (it ignores the real brand navy). From design-taste-frontend: the Design Read, the dials, the single-accent lock, the hero discipline (max 4 text elements, subtext ≤ 20 words), max 1 marquee, no em-dashes in site copy, no custom cursor, no eyebrow on every section, no "beige + brass + espresso" default palette (Bécam's real navy + gold avoids it naturally) and motion that must be motivated.

**Design Read:** *a local storefront site for a franchise bakery-café at a campus metro exit, for students, commuters and Thabor families, in a warm heritage-royal language (Angevin navy and gold) with a light Rennes accent, built with native CSS + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis on Vite/TS.*
**Dials:** DESIGN_VARIANCE **7**, MOTION_INTENSITY **8**, VISUAL_DENSITY **4**.

**Differentiation check against sibling projects:** Boulangerie Fleur de Sel uses « Tourage » (night 1h → dawn 6h30, salt-white #F5F6F3 + night #0F131B + gold #D2AE5C, Schibsted Grotesk). Bécam must **not** be a night/dawn concept, **not** near-black, and **not** grotesk-led. It is a **daylight, blue-field, serif-headed** site. Seize Heures Trente (a competitor) will get its own direction, and the two must not share a palette or concept.

### Concept: **« Lys & Hermine, derrière la verrière »**
*One-line idea:* **An Angevin maison opens its glass bakehouse at the foot of the Jules Ferry metro. The whole site is a verrière (a grid of glass panes in Bécam navy) through which you watch one Rennes day, 7 h to 20 h, from the first flûte to the last Choc'O Roi, with the fleur de lys of Anjou meeting the ermine of Rennes.**

Why it is *this* business and no other:
- **The verrière is real.** The shop's defining physical feature is the « grande verrière » onto the fournil (owner quote, RIA). The Jules Ferry station's architecture is also glass and concrete.
- **The day is real.** 7:00-20:00, every day, is its #1 competitive edge and a top review theme. The brand concept is literally « l'artisanat à toute heure de la journée ».
- **Lys & Hermine is real.** It is the first Bécam in Brittany: the fleur de lys sits on the Choc'O Roi and in "Lys d'Or" and "Délys des Rois", and the ermine chief sits on the Rennes arms.
- **Navy + gold is real.** The franchisor's two "couleurs fortes" (#023b58 / #c2a862).

### Personality keywords
Lumineux · artisanal-à-vue · royal sans être guindé · campus-friendly · pratique · généreux · Anjou-en-Bretagne.

### Typography
- **Display: Cormorant Garamond** (600 / 700, italic 500-600). This is a justified serif under design-taste rules: the brand is genuinely heritage and royal (Roi René, Plantagenêt, fleur de lys, tapestries) and already uses a serif (Merriweather) alongside Montserrat. Italic is for royal flourishes such as « *Choc'O Roi* » and « *à toute heure* ». Italic display lines need line-height ≥ 1.1 for descenders. It is not used by any sibling project so far.
- **Body / UI / prices: Montserrat** (400 / 500 / 600 / 700). This is the **real brand font**. Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for prices, hours and the day dial.
- Scale: hero `clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 5.2rem)` Cormorant 600, headline ≤ 2 lines. Section titles `clamp(1.9rem, 3.6vw, 3.2rem)`. Body 1rem/1.6 Montserrat 400, max 65ch. Small caps labels: Montserrat 600 0.75rem with 0.12em tracking, **at most 1 per 3 sections**.
- Self-host with `@fontsource/cormorant-garamond` and `@fontsource/montserrat` (verify package names), `font-display: swap`, subset latin + latin-ext (é, è, à, ç, œ, «»).
- French typography: non-breaking space before `: ; ! ?` and inside « ». Prices like « 3,90 € ». Hours like « 7 h - 20 h » (hyphen, no en/em dash in visible copy).

### Signature interaction: **« La verrière, 7 h → 20 h »** (the day dial)
A horizontal **day dial** (a gold fleur-de-lys handle sliding along a 7-20 h rule, with hours in tabular Montserrat) sits under the hero verrière and returns as a sticky mini-dial on `/la-journee` and `/carte`.
- **On load** it snaps to the **real current time in Rennes** (outside opening hours it rests at 20 h with « On rouvre demain à 7 h »).
- **Dragging, pressing arrow keys or scrolling the dial** does three things at once. (1) The verrière panes re-light with the day-part tint and the bakers' scene changes: 7 h scoring flûtes, 12 h croustillants on the counter, 16 h Choc'O Roi trays, 19 h last loaves, 20 h panes go navy and a single gold lys lights up. (2) A **« En vitrine maintenant »** shelf re-stocks with the products tagged for that day-part (from `dayparts.ts`), sliding in like trays. (3) A one-line caption gives the honest service of that hour (« 7 h : premières flûtes, café à emporter », « 12 h : formules snacking dès 7,90 € », « 18 h : dernière heure pour commander pour demain », « 20 h : fermeture, à demain »).
- Built as an accessible `<input type="range" min="7" max="20" step="0.25">` with custom styling, `aria-valuetext="16 h 30, goûter"`, plus 5 quick buttons (Matin, Midi, Goûter, Soir, Fermeture) for touch and reduced motion.
- **Second signature: « Le Choc'O Roi, couche par couche »** (pinned, scrubbed exploded view). **Layers follow the official composition** (Bakeronline: « Pâte sucrée Fleur de Lys / Sablé breton chocolat / Crème pâtissière Choc'O Roi »; press adds 65 % cocoa and Guérande salt): the **sablé breton chocolat** base lifts → the **crème pâtissière Choc'O Roi** (« chocolat 65 % », Bonjour Rennes) spreads → a few **Guérande fleur de sel** crystals fall (gold-white particles) → the **pâte sucrée disc lands and its fleur de lys is stamped** (scale 1.15 → 1, short "press" overshoot) → the round **boîte peuplier** lid closes (Flip). Each layer has a text label (the official words above) for screen readers and the reduced-motion version. A small toggle switches to the three individual flavours (caramel / chocolat / vanille: « Sablé Breton » + « crème pâtissière » of that flavour).
- **Third, small signature: the B-É-C-A-M values** (real, §5): five gold capitals that each unfold into their value (Bien-être, Écoute, Créativité, Accompagnement, Motivation) on hover/tap; used once, on `/la-maison`.

### Motion language
- **Libraries (check current docs, licences and caniuse before building):** GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger + SplitText + Flip (verify the current free-licence status), Lenis smooth scroll (disabled under reduced motion and on coarse pointers if janky), the **View Transitions API** for route changes (same-document `document.startViewTransition` in the small router, crossfade fallback), and CSS scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline: view()`) under `@supports` for simple reveals. Animate only `transform`, `opacity` and sparing `clip-path`.
- **Hero:** panes start "fogged" (`--verre` at 0.9 opacity with a blur on a pseudo-element). They clear in a staggered diagonal wipe (60 ms stagger, `expo.out`, 900 ms total). The headline rises by line with SplitText (mask reveal, 700 ms), then the dial handle slides from 7 h to the current time (1.2 s, `power3.inOut`) as a demonstration.
- **Scroll storytelling:**
  1. **« Une journée rue Jean Guéhenno »**: horizontal pan, pinned at `top top`, scrubbed. Six panes: 7 h fournil, 8 h métro & café, 12 h campus & formules, 16 h goûter & Choc'O Roi, 18 h commandes pour demain, 20 h la verrière s'éteint. The dial on top tracks progress.
  2. **Choc'O Roi exploded build** (above).
  3. **« D'Angers à Rennes »**: an SVG route line draws from Angers (2005) via Laval (70 km) to Rennes (22 mars 2025, 28e maison, première en Bretagne) as you scroll. Network milestones pop in as small gold lys markers.
  4. **The six métiers bento**: tiles slide up out of the "counter" with a staggered reveal.
- **Page transitions:** a **glass-door wipe**: a column of navy panes slides across (450 ms in / 350 ms out, `expo.inOut`), the new page fades up behind it. Product → product-sheet uses a Flip shared element on the illustration.
- **Micro-interactions:** buttons fill with a gold-leaf sweep from the cursor's entry side (directional hover); "Ajouter à ma liste" makes a tiny flour puff (6 particles, 400 ms) and the list counter ticks; price tags flip in when filters change; the status pill breathes once per minute when the shop is open (a real semantic state, the only "live" dot on the page); tapestry pattern subtly parallaxes (≤ 12 px) in the footer; the one marquee (customer words) reverses direction with scroll velocity.
- **Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):** no Lenis, no pinning, no scrub, no particles, no shader (static gradient). The verrière renders clear and lit for the *current* day-part. The day dial still works but swaps states instantly with a 150 ms opacity crossfade. The horizontal day becomes a vertical list of six panes. The Choc'O Roi shows fully assembled with a labelled layer diagram. Page transitions become a 150 ms fade. The marquee becomes a static wrapped word cloud. Everything stays readable and complete.
- **Performance:** first load ships CSS + hero SVG + fonts only; GSAP and ScrollTrigger load in the main bundle, the shader and Choc'O Roi timeline lazy-load on intersection. Target LCP < 2.5 s, CLS < 0.1. Grain/noise only on a fixed `pointer-events:none` layer.
